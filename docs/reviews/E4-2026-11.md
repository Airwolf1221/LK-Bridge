# E4-2026-11: the second independent review, before Arkona

An independent review of everything built since the mid-point review ([CR-2026-11](CR-2026-11.md), merged at `e14b867`):
C2d and C2d.1 (whole-set lifecycle, the status-tag guard, "an approval covers only what it listed", moves within the
Working and Archived areas), D1 and D1.1 (the calendar engine, chronology sync, `sync_timeline_event`, versions from the
`ETag`), D2 and D2-D3.1 (map features, pixels, top-level moves), D3 (the image pipeline and its public `/uploads/<id>`),
E1a and E1a.1 (snapshots, restores, the rehearsal, nightly full snapshots, retention and the collector), E1b (Google
sign-in, the rclone runner, verify, `offsite-restore.sh`, `/about` and `/privacy`), E2 (audits, "Propose a fix") and E3 and
E3b (the tool-call log, merged and hidden tools, the evaluation helper), on `main` at `226991b` (15 November 2026). It also
checked that CR's fixes still hold, built the backlog the session reports left (section 5), and wrote PLAN-v2 10.6's
concurrency acceptance tests (section 4).

**How it was done.** The reviewer did not write the code and treated every "verified", "tested" and "covered" in the
reports, the changelog, the ADRs and the code comments as a claim to check. Four separate reviewers, each in its own copy
of the repository and each given the code and an area rather than the reviewer's conclusions, tried to break: the public
surface (RV1), the guards through the new tools (RV2), data loss, restores and the collector (RV3), and failure, resume,
locks and budgets (RV4). Every finding below was reproduced by a test that asserts the safe behaviour and **fails on the
old code**; each fix makes it pass. The tests are in `test/e4/` (one file per finding, named after it) and beside the code
they test. **Nothing was run against a live system**: every test runs against the mock LegendKeeper in this repository;
the owner's LegendKeeper bridge connector, Google and every other live service were never called, not even for a read.
The sandbox tests of section 6 are written and dry-run against the mock, not run; the owner runs them.

**Severity**, as CR used it: critical — could lose or corrupt data, or pass canon or lifecycle approval; high — a wrong
result, a misleading state or a real security weakness; medium — cost or robustness; low — clarity.

### E4-01 (Critical): `restoreResource` was never judged by the lifecycle guard

- **Found by:** RV2, reading `src/lifecycle/guard.ts`: `restoreResource` was named only in `reshapes` and judged neither at
  entry nor in the lock; `restore_resource` checked only that the parent exists, and `restore_snapshot`'s undelete nothing.
  (1) An agent deletes a `[BWRK]` page of a Working set (an ordinary write); the owner promotes the set (the deleted page is
  not in that approval); `restore_resource` brings it back under the set's index, now in canon, still `[BWRK]`. The same
  brought a CANON index back inside the Archived area. (2) With no approval at all: delete the `[BWRK]` page and its now
  empty set index; `lk_api_write restoreResource` puts the page at the top level (LegendKeeper does so when the parent is
  gone); `moveResource` from the top level crosses no area, so it moves into canon.
- **Fix:** a restore is judged as a move from the area the page belongs in by its own state (prefix, status tag) to the
  area it lands in (its old parent's, or the top level); a move of a page whose own state is Working or Archived anywhere
  outside that area is refused, whatever it is moved from; every `deleteResource` keeps the page itself (name, parent,
  tags) as its journal pre-image (one read more per delete; `delete_resource`'s subtree record now carries the tags), so a
  restore can be judged; a page the bridge has no record of is judged as a crossing (ADR 0046 decision 4).
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv2-restore-area.test.ts` (4 tests: after a promotion, through `lk_api_write`, into the archive,
  the agent-alone route); `test/jobs/guards.test.ts` (the delete's pre-image).

### E4-02 (Critical): promoting one Core dragged unlisted Working pages into canon

- **Found by:** RV2. Since C2d.1 a Working character's specialist pages are made under its `[INDEX]`, which moves with the
  Core (ADR 0035 decision 10). The marker declared only the Core and the index, the guard judged the index's move and
  never what is beneath it, the proposal listed only the index's direct children, and nothing checked the subtree at
  approval: a `[BWRK]` page two levels down moved into canon unlisted, and one added after the proposal was taken along
  by the approval (ADR 0036 decision 5).
- **Fix:** promoting a single Core whose index holds any other page, at any depth, is refused (`SET_HAS_OTHER_PAGES`,
  naming them, pointing to `subtree: true` or moving them out), and the job checks inside the lock that the index still
  holds only the Core (a `subtree` check). This changes ADR 0035 decision 10's documented behaviour: ADR 0046 decision 2.
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv2-promote-drag.test.ts` (both tests); `test/lifecycle/characters.test.ts` (the test that pinned
  the old drag now pins the refusal and the move-out-first route).

### E4-03 (Critical): verify left full snapshots partial for good, and retention deleted them

- **Found by:** RV3. Objects are deduplicated, so one damaged object (an unchanged page's piece) made verify mark every
  full snapshot that used it partial (`markDamaged`); the next nightly run repaired the object, but nothing set the
  snapshots back. Retention keeps a partial full snapshot only for the first 30 days and never counts it as "the newest
  complete", so every weekly full went, and with the newest full over 30 days old, every full; the collector then removed
  their objects.
- **Fix:** verify sets a snapshot back to complete when its manifest had nothing missing and none of its findings is open
  (`clearRepaired`); retention keeps the newest full snapshot of any state when none is complete.
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv3-retention.test.ts` (a repaired weekly snapshot kept; the only full snapshot never deleted).

### E4-04 (High): look-alike status tags and Status values swapped for the genuine one

- **Found by:** RV2. The guards fold look-alikes (CR-12, CR-14) so a disguised value cannot be added unseen, but the fold
  also hid the swap: add `СANON` (Cyrillic С) beside `CANON` (no change: the page "already has CANON"), then remove
  `CANON` (no change: the look-alike still counts). `set_facts Status "Authoritаtive"` (Cyrillic а) was written with no
  canonical approval. The bridge still treated the page as Authoritative; LegendKeeper and anything matching exact text did
  not.
- **Fix:** any status-tag spelling added or removed is a change, as is a protected value whose exact spelling changes while
  its folded form stays (`src/lifecycle/guard.ts` `statusTagChanges`, `src/facts/canonical.ts`).
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv2-status-tag-swap.test.ts` (3 tests).

### E4-05 (High): a full snapshot's pieces dated at the run's end

- **Found by:** RV3. A nightly run reads page P; an agent then writes P and its change snapshot records the new content;
  the run finishes later, and its older copy of P counted as newer. From then on "the state at" any later snapshot came
  from the old copy: a restore to it silently reverted the write, and the history misordered P.
- **Fix:** each page entry records when it was read (`capturedAt`, an optional manifest field: the store's format is
  unchanged for E1b), and its pieces are indexed at that time.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv3-history.test.ts`.

### E4-06 (High): abandoning a running full snapshot wrote an empty, complete one

- **Found by:** RV3. `abandonRun` deleted the run's page rows while the run carried on; it then found no entries and
  indexed a full manifest with 0 pages, `complete`, which became the newest complete full snapshot (kept by retention, read
  by the rehearsal and the audits).
- **Fix:** the run checks its own state before each page and before its manifest, and stops when abandoned.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv3-abandon.test.ts`.

### E4-07 (High): a restored image was re-encoded

- **Found by:** RV3. `image-restore.ts` converted the stored bytes again: a PNG or JPEG came back as WebP at quality 90,
  colour profile and metadata dropped (the rehearsal, by contrast, uploaded the bytes as stored).
- **Fix:** the snapshot store's bytes are uploaded exactly, the header read for their facts, nothing decoded
  (`asStoredImage`; ADR 0046 decision 5).
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv3-image.test.ts`.

### E4-08 (High): `update_timeline_event` dropped an event's styling

- **Found by:** RV3. When a range becomes a single date (LegendKeeper cannot take an end away) or the event moves to
  another age, the event is deleted and made again, from its name, dates, detail, lane and link only: its colour, icon,
  image, hidden and calendar flags were lost, though only the date was asked for.
- **Fix:** the new event keeps every writable field of the old one (icons in their write form), then the changes.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv3-chronology.test.ts`.

### E4-09 (High): a disk error during an upload stopped the bridge

- **Found by:** RV1. The upload's temporary file had no `error` listener: a failed write (disk full, quota, I/O) was an
  uncaught exception and the whole process exited, with every running job. The answer also carried the raw `ENOSPC`
  message, the slot stayed `receiving`, and the `.part` file was left (the sweep skips them).
- **Fix:** the stream's errors, `end` and `rename` go through the receive's own failure path: the slot is spent, the file
  removed, and the answer is a generic 507 `storage` refusal.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv1-upload-tempfile.test.ts`.

### E4-10 (High): backup protections switched off without re-authentication

- **Found by:** RV1. `PUT /offsite/settings` and `POST /maintenance/:id/disable` needed only the session: a stolen session
  could switch the monthly verify off, push the stale-copy warning to 365 days, shorten how long removed pieces stay on
  Drive to one day, change the folder, or disable the nightly full snapshot and its verify.
- **Fix:** each of those needs the re-authentication of the last 5 minutes; the dashboard asks for the password (ADR 0046
  decision 3).
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv1-offsite-settings-reauth.test.ts` (both tests).

### E4-11 (High): a whole-set job overwrote app edits after a hold

- **Found by:** RV4. CR-06 re-runs a job's checks when it takes its lock again with nothing sent; once a write has been
  sent, "the writes after it carry their own checks". A set's later rename-and-tag writes carried none: archiving a set,
  writes paused after the first write, a tag added to a later page in the app, writes resumed: the job ended verified and
  the app's tag was gone.
- **Fix:** each lifecycle `updateResource` checks inside the lock that the page's name and tags are still as planned
  (`expectCurrent` state `resource`, compared with the read the write already makes: no extra request).
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv4-set-hold.test.ts`.

### E4-12 (High): two `update_resource` calls at once lost a tag

- **Found by:** this review's concurrency test (section 4). LegendKeeper does not version a resource and `tags` replaces the
  list: two `update_resource` tag additions at once both answered "written", verified, and one tag was lost; an app edit
  between the plan and the write was overwritten the same way.
- **Fix:** `update_resource`'s write checks the name and tags inside the lock as E4-11 does: the second is a reported
  conflict.
- **Test:** `test/e4/concurrency.test.ts` ("every answer is written or an explicit conflict").

### E4-13 (High): a stopped chronology change misreported

- **Found by:** RV4. The job's labels include the bridge's own `record` steps, counted as LegendKeeper writes: with the
  first `insertBlock` refused, `create_timeline_event` answered `WRITTEN_IN_PART` ("Written in part: record") and "Do not
  repeat this request", while ADR 0038's resume is exactly "repeat the same request".
- **Fix:** `record` steps are the bridge's own; a change with a record says to repeat the same request to finish it.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv4-chronology-report.test.ts`.

### E4-14 (High): a stopped recreate said the page "already matches"

- **Found by:** RV4. A recreate stopped at its first property (or event): `scope: "deleted"` again is `NOT_DELETED`;
  `scope: "sidebar"` answered `nothing_to_do`, "The page already matches the snapshot here", while its own diff showed the
  whole sidebar missing; `scope: "page"` listed every document twice and said the same.
- **Fix:** "already matches" only when nothing is listed as not restored; otherwise the hint says the page is not as it
  was at the snapshot and what is not restored. **Open:** finishing a stopped recreate (section 7).
- **Tests:** `test/e4/rv4-recreate-resume.test.ts` (the honest answer; the resume is marked open in the file).

### E4-15 (High): proposing a split again duplicated the text

- **Found by:** RV4. A split whose `replaceBlock` failed after its inserts leaves the text twice, as designed; the finding
  stays open; proposing the fix again inserted the parts a second time ("one | two | three | two | three").
- **Fix:** a proposal finds parts already after the paragraph and only finishes the split; the in-lock check refuses a plan
  that would insert parts that are already there.
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv4-split-resume.test.ts`.

### E4-16 (High): a chronology resume replayed a stale plan

- **Fix:** an inserted row that is there but no longer as written refuses the resume (`CHRONOLOGY_RESUME_CONFLICT`).
- **Test:** `test/e4/rv4-chronology-stale.test.ts`.

## 3. What was checked and found sound

So later sessions know what has been reviewed. Each was checked by reading the code and, where noted, by tests.

**CR's fixes still hold.** CR-01 (delete pre-images; a lane's now holds its events), CR-02 (the `property` guard), CR-06
(`recheckOnRelock`), CR-08 (only a job's own chained row writes skip the in-lock judgement), CR-09 and CR-10 (live subtree
listings), CR-11 (a Status mention), CR-12 and CR-14 (folding, now with the exact-spelling check of E4-04), CR-13 (moves
from LegendKeeper's parent), CR-15 (no new route renames, moves or deletes a lifecycle index; a recreate under an index's
name is refused), CR-16 (a `[BSRC]` recreate is canonical), CR-17, CR-19 (restores and fixes run as owner-approved jobs).
Every CR test still passes. E4-01 is a kind of move none of them judged.

**Guards through the new tools.** The dashboard's owner-forced proposals need the system principal, a `ui` origin and no
approval: only dashboard code (snapshot routes, staging, audit fixes, the chronology fix) forces them, agents' restores
carry their own principal, and a follow-up restore keeps it. `lk_api_batch` and the per-op tools map only
`allowUnsupportedLoss` and `confirm` into step options; `bridge.*` steps need `internal`; proposals keep bridge-set options
through an allow-list; `dropIfRefused` drops only icon fields, after the guards. `bindBody` replaces only a body, checked
against the schema, before the in-lock guards see it. `judgedInLock` only skips version capture at proposal time. Deleting
a map of an Authoritative page stays canonical (a blank tab turned into a map in the same batch is caught). Audit fixes stay
under the same parent, refuse status tags and make protected facts canonical. Images: the gate lets only staging uploads
through at `propose`; placement is the proposal. The evaluation helper is owner-only and reads names and sizes.

**Data and restores.** Restores stay in their project (a snapshot of another project is refused) and a recreate's parent
is inside the repository and outside the lifecycle areas. The rehearsal checks `REHEARSAL_SAME_PROJECT` before
`REHEARSAL_NOT_SANDBOX`, keys projects by their LegendKeeper id, and `assertInsideRoot` judges creates by parent, assets by
folder and everything else by resource. The paragraph split keeps marks, ids and neighbours and refuses icon paragraphs.
Chronology table writes are block-level, guarded and journalled; a same-age replace keeps the other cells. Map cascades
journal every removed feature. Asset deletes keep their bytes or are refused, and the collector keeps every kept reference.
The collector runs under the exclusive lock with a one-hour grace period and keeps what manifests, running runs and kept
references name; `reindex` runs at start-up. The canonical form keeps block ids and ranks and strips only string `version`
keys.

**The public surface.** The upload capability is 256 bits, stored as its SHA-256 and compared in constant time, with one
generic refusal; the move to `receiving` is one conditional `UPDATE`; the binding covers project, connection, length (a
body stops at length + 1), SHA-256 and magic-byte type; the request log strips query strings, logs no headers and redacts
`authorization`; idle and total timeouts drop slow bodies; ids are checked before the database; files are 0600 in 0700
directories; sharp's pixel and frame limits are read from the header first. The Google sign-in: a 256-bit `state` bound to
the session's hash, used once, 10 minutes; PKCE S256; a fixed redirect URI and a fixed 303 target; the `drive.file` check
with revocation; the refresh token only in the secret store; errors redacted. `/about` and `/privacy` set no cookie, carry
the strict CSP and escape the contact email. The tool-call log stores codes, never messages, values only for allow-listed
names, and cuts argument names to 64 characters. Every admin route added since CR is inside `/api/admin` behind the session
guard (Origin, CSRF, `Sec-Fetch-Site`), and `test/admin/auth.test.ts` lists the routes from the running app, so it walks the
new ones; no GET changes state.

**Concurrency and locks** (RV4's `test/e4/rv4-locks.test.ts`): 16 jobs at once, three holding 20 overlapping job-level
locks each given in a different order, 12 single writes and a held document, with writes paused and resumed mid-run: all
finished, no deadlock. The engine sorts every job-level lock and refuses a write its locks do not cover; waiters are served
first come, first served. A chronology Main count is never counted twice after a lost answer (its before and after are
recorded when the record begins). Map cascades resume with what is left. A 500 after a write applied is re-read before the
step fails.

**Budgets** (RV4's `rv4-budget-night.test.ts` and `rv4-arkona-snapshot.test.ts`, both passing):

- one simulated night on Arkona (fake clock, published limits, default floors): the 3,400-read full snapshot, a link sweep
  (1,600 reads) and a tree check (1,500) at 4 in flight each, while agents sent 6 reads every 15 s and a 40-read, 20-write
  job every 5 minutes: P3 peaked at 150 a minute (its floor), at most 2,518 in any hour; all traffic peaked at 168 a minute
  (limit 300) and 4,458 an hour (limit 6,000); 12,980 requests in 6 hours against 60,000 a day; P3 jobs share one window
  per tier, so they cannot overshoot together; P1 dispatch delay 0 ms at the 99th percentile and the maximum (n = 4,320);
  P2's maximum 460 ms (write pacing). The snapshot finished in 225 minutes (about 63 alone), yielding 72 times;
- an Arkona-sized mock on the real stack (1,493 pages under the root, 3,514 resources): the full snapshot took 3,011 reads
  (2.02 a page); 400 agent `get_resource` calls during it: median 8 ms, 95th percentile 19 ms, maximum 239 ms (10 ms
  median unloaded). With the key-hour limit at 4,000 the snapshot stopped at exactly 2,000 (the P3 floor), waited, and
  finished with nothing read twice;
- the off-site runner imports nothing of `src/lk` or `src/jobs`: it makes no LegendKeeper request. Audits read snapshots;
  only the owner's "Re-check now" and "Propose a fix" read LegendKeeper, at P1.

## 4. Concurrency (PLAN-v2 10.6)

1. **Many simultaneous writes to one page, mixed tools** (`test/e4/concurrency.test.ts`): five `write_page` appends, three
   `set_facts`, three `update_resource` tag additions, two `create_map_feature` pins and two `replaceBoardContent` with one
   version, all at once from four connections. Every answer is written (verified) or an explicit conflict, a conflict never
   claims a write, and every written change is in LegendKeeper at the end; of the two board writes with one version at
   most one goes in. This test found E4-12. (C1's `test/pages/concurrency.test.ts` already covers `write_page` alone,
