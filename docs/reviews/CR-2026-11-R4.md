### The narrative exemptions (scope 1)

### R4-01 (Critical): an Authoritative page moved into Narratives, directly, by an agent at scope `write`

- **What:** a page is narrative by where it sits (everything under the Narratives index; `src/narrative/scope.ts`
  `inNarratives`), and no guard knew Narratives existed: the lifecycle guard's areas were Working and Archived only, the
  canon guard has no move rule, and `move_resource` has no check of its own. One direct write (`move_resource`,
  `lk_api_write` or `lk_api_batch` `moveResource`, `lk_op_moveResource`) moved a `[BSRC]` CANON page, or a CANON index
  with its pages, into a story; it was then refused by the lifecycle tools (`NARRATIVE_NO_LIFECYCLE`), refused by
  `arkona_brief` (`NARRATIVE_NOT_AUTHORITY`), exempt from the appraisal (`NARRATIVE_EXEMPT`) and from the lifecycle
  audit, and left out of the facts index (Rashad al-Khalid's birth gone from `arkona_as_of`). The reverse move made a
  chapter canon evidence: the consistency check reported "Contradictory: Leila Darvish's birth is c. 4A 640 on her Core,
  but 4A 700 on Chapter 1 - Salt Tide". The move was on the Review page, revertible; nothing else said so.
- **Reproduced by:** `test/review/r4-narrative/move.test.ts` NA-01 to NA-04, NA-06, NA-09, NA-37;
  `audit.test.ts` NA-36, NA-43; effects NA-07, NA-08.
- **Fixed:** Narratives is an area the lifecycle guard knows, by the id the narrative tools resolve (`src/lifecycle/guard.ts`
  `NarrativeArea`, `src/narrative/scope.ts` `narrativeArea`; none when missing or ambiguous, so every page keeps its
  lifecycle). On every route, and again inside the lock with the page's place and its new parent read live, a page or
  subtree moved into Narratives (or created, copied or restored there outside the narrative tools), or moved out of it,
  is a proposal at every scope, canonical when any page of it carries a lifecycle signal (`src/narrative/signals.ts`:
  a `[BSRC]`, `[BWRK]` or `[BARC]` prefix read loosely, a status tag in any spelling, a lifecycle Status); the subtree is
  listed live as a cascade is (at most 100 pages, fail closed). The guard can now ask for an ordinary proposal
  (`src/jobs/write-guard.ts`), shown as `proposedBecause`. NA-22, NA-23, NA-41 and NA-42 relied on the agent's move
  going through; the lead rebuilt their set-up as the owner filing the canon in the app, so each still tests its
  defence. That set-up found the rest of NA-43: canon filed under Narratives (by the owner, or by approving the
  canonical proposal) still left the facts index, so `arkona_as_of` lost Rashad's birth; a page under Narratives whose
  name carries a lifecycle prefix now stays evidence (`src/narrative/authority.ts`), and `narrative_lifecycle_page`
  reports it (R4-08). ADR 0065 amendment 2.

### R4-02 (Critical): the narrative audit's tags fix stripped CANON on an ordinary approval

- **What:** the fix's job declares its pages as having no lifecycle (a marker `to: null`), and the lifecycle guard's
  `declares()` took that as declaring any change, though its own comment says `to: null` covers only where a page is
  placed; the fix's tags are `narrativeTags`, which drop CANON, Working and Archived. On a `[BSRC]` CANON page in a
  story (or a CANON index in a continuity), the ordinary Approve removed CANON.
- **Reproduced by:** `test/review/r4-narrative/audit.test.ts` NA-29, NA-30, NA-41.
- **Fixed:** `declares()` takes a marker's `to: null` as declaring where a page is placed and the first state of a page the
  same job creates (judged inside the lock from the job's own create steps, so a restart keeps it), never a change of
  an existing page's Status, prefix or tags; the narrative tags fix only adds tags, every tag the page holds kept by its
  exact spelling (`src/audits/checks/narrative.ts`, `src/audits/fixes.ts`).

### R4-03 (High): narrative `create_resource` set lifecycle signals directly

- **Reproduced by:** `test/review/r4-narrative/tools.test.ts` NA-18, NA-19, NA-39.
- **Fixed:** `create_resource` under Narratives refuses a Status in `facts` and any tag that folds to a status or

### R4-04 (High): the Narratives index was found by name, and nothing protected the name

### R4-20 (Critical): a relation's birth or death was read as the named character's

- **What:** the name nearest the verb governed it, whatever stood before it: "The daughter of Rashad al-Khalid was born
  on Camue 1st, 4A 401" was Rashad's birth (a Contradictory finding), so were "Layla bint Rashad al-Khalid was born…"
  and a chronology row "The heir of @Rashad al-Khalid is born"; "Karl Tobias von Hohenfalk was born in 3A 150" was
  Tobias's (born after his own death). Downstream (AS-92) the audit reported it, the brief repeated it, and the owner's
  pick of Rashad's true birth, approved, rewrote the annals' correct sentence about his daughter.
- **Reproduced by:** `test/review/r4-asof/attribution.test.ts` AS-39 to AS-42, AS-51, AS-84; `pick.test.ts` AS-39,
  AS-92.

## 3. What was checked and found sound

Read by the lead, beside the passes; each item was checked against the code and, where a test pins it, the test.

- **The tool lists (scope 5), apart from R4-51 to R4-53.** The watch (`src/mcp/tool-list.ts`) is poked by every change
  of the packs, the projects served and the policy store (a pack's rules or approval, a profile's scope or low-level
  switch, and now a connection's profile), compares a fingerprint of what would be listed, and only when it moved has
  the transport send `notifications/tools/list_changed` to every connected 2026-07-28 session listening for it; each
  then asks for its own list, worked out afresh for its caller. A change that leaves every list as it was sends
  nothing. A 2025-era (stateless) session, which no push reaches, is told on its next request when its list differs
  from the one its connection was last given (`tellListChanged`, kept per connection's principal: two sessions of one
  connection share that notice; a limitation of stateless serving, recorded in ADR 0066 A2, not a finding). A
  list is what an agent is shown, not what it may do: every call is judged by the caller's live scope at the call
  (`src/mcp/server.ts`) and by the tool's own checks and the policy (a pack tool reads the approved version at every
  call: LD-01's calls to the tools wrongly listed were refused), so a notice that is late or missed lets nothing run
  that could not run anyway (the escalation test now attacks the registered tools a list leaves out as well).

  culture's expectations, and never states that two pages are the same person (no inference, as ADR 0063 says). "Allow
  this name" (`src/audits/service.ts` `allowName`) allows only a `governance_external_name` finding's name; it adds the
  name to the audit settings' allowed names (merged with those already there, ignoring case), closes the open findings
  of the same name, and is audited (`audits.allow_name`). Decision 3 (near misses) had landed for whole names and for
  runs that resolve as no name; it had not for a run that resolved through a one-word name (R4-54, fixed).
